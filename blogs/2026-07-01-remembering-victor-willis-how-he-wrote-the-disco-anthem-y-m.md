– Victor Willis, the lead vocalist and primary songwriter for the Village People, died yesterday at the age of 74, his wife announced in a statement this morning. The Library inducted “Y.M.C.A.” into the National Recording Registry in 2020 and I caught up with him for a delightful phone interview. He politely recounted, in detail, how he had written one of the most iconic songs in American pop...
